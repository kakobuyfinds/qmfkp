A simple research routine

Less scrolling.
Better notes.

Turn JoyaGoo finds into a small, readable shortlist. Keep a question beside every missing detail so you know what to resolve next.

  1. Choose the job the item must do.

    For sneaker comparison, decide whether you need everyday footwear or a particular activity. For a hoodie fabric comparison, note the stated composition and the warmth you want.

  2. Save the source and selected option.

    Record the original marketplace link, seller name and product ID. Add the exact color variant and size; a saved image alone is an incomplete reference.

  3. Separate evidence from assumptions.

    Label seller photos clearly. Use a QC photo checklist for images of the actual item, and write down any measurement or condition question the pictures leave unanswered.

  4. Review the order before paying.

    Complete a total landed cost check and read the current return policy. Confirm that the destination listing still matches your saved source record.

The measurement desk

A practical JoyaGoo size guide.

Compare like with like. Record units and ask how each measurement was taken.

What to record before choosing a size
Category Useful measurements Question to resolve
Shoes Foot length, stated size system and available internal measurements. Does the shoe size conversion match this specific model and listing?
T-Shirts & hoodies Garment chest width, shoulder width, sleeve and body length. Are these garment measurements or a recommended body size?
Jackets Chest, shoulder, sleeve and length with the intended layer underneath. Does the jacket layering fit leave the room you need?
Pants & shorts Waist, rise, hip, inseam and leg opening. Does the pants inseam measurement suit your preferred break or length?
Sets & jerseys Top and bottom dimensions separately; jersey chest and body length. Can you choose each piece separately, and is the jersey fit close or relaxed?
Underwear & accessories Waist or relevant circumference, dimensions and stated material. What hygiene exclusions, size limits or closure adjustments apply?

Make currency comparisons clear.

Keep the original currency beside any estimate. A CNY to USD estimate needs a date and exchange-rate assumption; confirm the actual conversion at checkout.

Keep shipping separate.

For parcel planning, distinguish domestic shipping from international shipping. Record whether a quote uses actual weight, volumetric weight or another calculation.

Keep the record useful.

A mobile-friendly shortlist needs a source link, variant, measurement notes and a last-checked date. Mark unavailable listings instead of treating old screenshots as stock confirmation.

Before the next click.

Quick answers about links, photos and the information a shopping list can—and cannot—provide.

What is a JoyaGoo spreadsheet?

A JoyaGoo spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize finds and shopping links associated with JoyaGoo. Useful entries record the source item, selected variant, measurements and information that still needs checking. This page is a guide, not a downloadable spreadsheet or live inventory.

Where do the links on this page go?

The ten category links open the specified CAFANS category pages. Every other navigation link opens the CAFANS homepage. These links do not place an order or open a JoyaGoo checkout.

How should I check JoyaGoo product links?

Compare the destination domain, original marketplace item ID and selected color and size. For a dead product link or an unexpected redirect, locate the original source record and verify it again before continuing.

What should I look for in JoyaGoo QC photos?

First establish whether the images are seller photos, previous-order photos or photos of your own item. Check the chosen variant, visible condition, labels, seams and requested measurements. Photos alone do not establish authenticity or guarantee fit.

Is a listed product price the final delivered cost?

Do not treat a listing price as a delivered quote. Confirm the current item amount, currency, domestic delivery, service and payment charges, international shipping and any applicable import charges before paying.
